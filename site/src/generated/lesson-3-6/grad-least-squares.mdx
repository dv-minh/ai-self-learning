$$
\nabla L = \mathbf{X}\T(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{y})
$$

$$
\nabla L = \mathbf{0} \;\Rightarrow\; \mathbf{X}\T\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{X}\T\mathbf{y} \;\Rightarrow\; \mathbf{w} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{7}{6} \\ \frac{3}{2} \end{bmatrix}
$$
