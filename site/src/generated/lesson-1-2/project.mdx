$$
\mathbf{u} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\mathbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ -4 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\frac{\mathbf{u}\T\mathbf{v}}{\mathbf{v}\T\mathbf{v}} = \frac{5}{41} = \frac{5}{41}
$$

$$
\mathrm{proj}_{\mathbf{v}}\mathbf{u} = \frac{5}{41}\cdot \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ -4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{25}{41} \\ -\frac{20}{41} \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\mathbf{u} - \mathrm{proj}_{\mathbf{v}}\mathbf{u} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{16}{41} \\ \frac{20}{41} \end{bmatrix},\quad \left(\mathbf{u} - \mathrm{proj}_{\mathbf{v}}\mathbf{u}\right)\T\mathbf{v} = 0
$$

Phần dư vuông góc với $\mathbf{v}$ (tích vô hướng bằng $0$), đúng như kỳ vọng.
