Với $k = 3$, $\mathbf{u} = (-1, -3)\T$, $\mathbf{v} = (1, 3)\T$ và $\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$:

$$
T(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}) = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix},\qquad T(\mathbf{u})+T(\mathbf{v}) = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
T(k\mathbf{u}) = \begin{bmatrix} -21 \\ -12 \end{bmatrix},\qquad k\,T(\mathbf{u}) = \begin{bmatrix} -21 \\ -12 \end{bmatrix}
$$
