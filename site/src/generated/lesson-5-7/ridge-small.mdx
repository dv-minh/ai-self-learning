$$
x = (1, 2, 3),\ y = (2, 3, 5):\quad \sum xy = 23,\ \sum x^2 = 14
$$

$$
w^\star = \dfrac{\sum x_i y_i}{\sum x_i^2 + \lambda}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{cc} \lambda & w^\star \\ \hline 0 & \frac{23}{14} \\ 2 & \frac{23}{16} \\ 14 & \frac{23}{28} \\ 100 & \frac{23}{114} \end{array}
$$

$\lambda$ càng lớn, $w^\star$ càng bị co về $0$.
