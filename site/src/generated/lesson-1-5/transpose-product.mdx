$$
\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 4 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -3 \\ -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
(\mathbf{AB})\T = \begin{bmatrix} -4 & 1 \\ -11 & 6 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B}\T\mathbf{A}\T = \begin{bmatrix} -4 & 1 \\ -11 & 6 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Hai vế bằng nhau, đúng như tính chất $(\mathbf{AB})\T = \mathbf{B}\T\mathbf{A}\T$.
