Với $k = -2$, $\mathbf{u} = (2, 2, -1)\T$, $\mathbf{v} = (2, -4, 5)\T$:

$$
k(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}) = \begin{bmatrix} -8 \\ 4 \\ -8 \end{bmatrix},\qquad k\mathbf{u}+k\mathbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix} -4 \\ -4 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix} -4 \\ 8 \\ -10 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -8 \\ 4 \\ -8 \end{bmatrix}
$$
